Training Program

A bookkeeping training program built from 20+ years of real-world experience. Self-paced. Practical.

How It Works

Learn by doing. Not by watching.

Step-by-Step Lessons

Each module is a structured, text-based lesson. Read at your own pace, follow numbered steps, and reference back anytime. No scrubbing through videos.

Screenshot Walkthroughs

Every QBO and software task comes with annotated screenshots. See exactly where to click, what to enter, and how the screen should look — step by step.

Checklists Per Module

Each lesson ends with a practical checklist. Tick off each task as you complete it. Make sure nothing gets missed before moving to the next module.

Practical Exercises

Real-world scenarios at the end of each module. Reconcile a sample bank statement, generate a P&L, set up a chart of accounts. Learn by actually doing the work.

Downloadable Cheat Sheets

One-page PDF quick references for each module. Keep them at your desk for real-world use — journal entry types, reconciliation steps, QBO shortcuts.

Lifetime Access

No expiry. No subscription. Access the lessons whenever you need them — during the course, on the job, or years later as a reference.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need any prior accounting experience?

No. Module 01 starts from the very fundamentals — the accounting equation, debits and credits, and how transactions flow. If you can do basic math, you can start here.

Do I need to buy QuickBooks Online?

You'll need access to QBO for the practical exercises. We walk you through setting up a free trial account in Module 02, so you don't need to purchase anything upfront.

How long does the course take to complete?

Self-study students typically complete the program in 8–12 weeks at a comfortable pace. The intensive mentorship runs over 12 weeks with weekly sessions.

Is the QBO certification included?

The course prepares you for the QuickBooks Online certification exam. The exam itself is taken through Intuit and has a separate fee. We guide you through scheduling and preparing for it.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, contact us for a full refund — no questions asked.
